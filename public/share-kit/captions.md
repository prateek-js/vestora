## LinkedIn

*(pairs with `linkedin-card.png` — also the site's live share card)*

Most of us hold the same money twice over: a deposit here, a fund there, a
home loan running quietly in the background. Each one made sense when we
bought it. Together, nobody quite knows what they say.

Vestora puts all of it on one screen — FDs, mutual funds, stocks, gold, and
your home loan — and shows what your money actually looks like when it's
weighed together. Then it helps you rebalance it, step by step, without
commissions and without anyone selling you anything.

It runs entirely in your browser. Everything stays on your device; nothing is
sent anywhere.

Take the guided tour: vestora-wealth.ai.studio/demo/

## Product Hunt

**Gallery line** *(one per frame, used as the gallery caption)*

- `ph-problem.png` — Nine investments. Nobody looking at them together.
- `ph-beam.png` — See the honest shape of your money. Rebalance without commissions.
- `ph-rebalance.png` — Try the guided rebalance: what to move, where it goes, what changes.

**First comment (maker's):**

We built Vestora because the honest version of "check your portfolio" needs
every holding on one screen — including the home loan most tools ignore. It's
a browser app: no sign-up, no backend, nothing leaves your device. The
rebalance plan it suggests is one you could execute yourself at your bank or
broker tomorrow. We charge nothing and sell nothing. The guided walk-through
takes about two minutes: vestora-wealth.ai.studio/demo/
