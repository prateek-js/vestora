## Quick Intro Paragraphs

### LinkedIn Intro
Most Indian households don’t have a savings problem — they have a visibility problem. A maturing Fixed Deposit in one bank, Regular & Direct mutual funds across multiple apps, gold, NPS Tier-I, and an 8.55% home loan running quietly in the background each made sense on the day you picked them, yet nobody ever weighs them together on the same scale. I built **Vestora Wealth** (`vestorawealths.app`) to fix that: a free, zero-commission **Indian portfolio rebalancing calculator** and **private on-device wealth tracker** that puts your FDs, 40,000+ AMFI Direct-Growth mutual funds, stocks, gold/SGBs, NPS, RBI G-Secs, and home loan side by side, shows real post-tax FD vs home loan prepayment math and ₹1.25L equity LTCG tax harvesting audits, and builds a step-by-step rebalance plan right inside your browser — where everything stays on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

### Product Hunt Intro
**Vestora Wealth** (`vestorawealths.app`) is a free, zero-commission **Indian portfolio rebalancing calculator** and **private on-device wealth tracker** — built to weigh everything you actually hold on one honest scale. Instead of splitting your attention across bank portals, broker apps, and loan statements, Vestora puts your Fixed Deposits, 40,000+ Direct-Growth AMFI mutual funds, stocks, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), NPS Tier-I, RBI G-Secs, and home loan on one screen to show the real post-tax math behind every move (including when prepaying an 8.55% home loan beats renewing a 7.2% FD, plus 30/60/90-day FD Maturity Radar and ₹1.25L LTCG tax harvesting audits). No sign-up, no backend database, no commissions: it runs 100% locally in your browser.
