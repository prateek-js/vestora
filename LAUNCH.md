## Quick Intro Paragraphs

### LinkedIn Intro
Most Indian households don’t have a savings problem — they have a visibility problem. An FD sitting in one bank, mutual funds across two apps, a little gold, and an 8.55% home loan running quietly in the background each made sense on the day you picked them, yet nobody ever weighs them together on the same scale. I built **Vestora Wealth** to fix that: one quiet, zero-commission workspace that puts your FDs, Direct mutual funds, stocks, gold, NPS, RBI G-Secs, and home loan side by side, shows their true post-tax trade-offs, and builds a step-by-step rebalance plan right inside your browser — where everything stays on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

### Product Hunt Intro
**Vestora Wealth** is a calm, zero-commission portfolio rebalance planner for Indian households — built to weigh everything you actually hold on one honest scale. Instead of splitting your attention across bank portals, broker apps, and loan statements, Vestora puts your FDs, 40,000+ Direct-Growth AMFI mutual funds, stocks, gold, NPS, RBI G-Secs, and home loan on one screen to show the real post-tax math behind every move (including when prepaying an 8.55% home loan beats renewing a 7.2% FD). No sign-up, no backend database, no commissions: it runs entirely in your browser, so everything stays on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.
