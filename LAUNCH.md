### Description (Short + Full)
Vestora Wealth is a 100% private, zero-signup portfolio intelligence and holistic rebalance simulator built for multi-asset retail and NRI investors.

Start on the homepage with our **Interactive Wealth Story Game & Short Animated Movie** — where an investor juggling multiple investments and an active home loan visits conflicting finance sites, commission-driven sales advisors, and fee-heavy wealth managers before **Vestora Wealth** arrives to solve everything fairly on one screen — then jump straight into your live workspace:

- **Holistic Rebalance & Market Explorer**: Compare post-tax yields across **40,000+ live AMFI Mutual Funds**, **Kuvera category-ranked Direct-Growth funds**, **live NPS Tier-I pensions (`npsnav.in`)**, **live GoI G-Secs & T-Bills**, **US Treasuries**, **24K Gold**, and **Home Loan Principal Prepayment** with exact interest & months saved.
- **Local Command Chat**: Add, edit, top-up, withdraw, simulate, import, and export holdings via plain English or guided quick-action chips — 100% locally on your device with zero external AI calls.
- **Smart Diversification Engine**: Adjust your goal (`Conservative`, `Balanced`, `Growth`), tax bracket (`0–30%`), and home loan rate (`% p.a.`) to generate tailored rebalance plans backed by live yields.
- **Maturity Radar & Tax Audit**: Catch matured/expiring FDs, SGBs, and Bonds across 30/60/90 days, track Section 80C / 80CCD(1B) NPS headroom, and harvest annual equity LTCG exemptions.
- **Zero Server Storage**: No sign-up, no accounts, and zero cloud tracking. Import Excel (`.xlsx`), CAMS/KFintech CAS, or CSV and export printable PDF reports or spreadsheets anytime.
