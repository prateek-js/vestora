### Description (Short + Full)
Vestora Wealth is a 100% private, zero-signup portfolio intelligence and holistic rebalance simulator built for multi-asset retail and NRI investors.

Start by walking through our **Interactive Street Game & Short Animated Story** — where a stressed investor walks past siloed bank counters, government pension windows, and commission-heavy mutual fund agents before stepping inside Vestora Wealth — then jump straight into your live financial cockpit:

- **Holistic Rebalance & Market Explorer**: Break partial or full capital from any Savings Account, FD, Stock, or Mutual Fund and split across **40,000+ live AMFI Mutual Funds**, **Kuvera category-ranked Direct-Growth funds**, **live NPS Tier-I pensions (`npsnav.in`)**, **live GoI G-Secs & T-Bills**, **US Treasuries**, **24K Gold**, or **Home Loan Principal Prepayment** with exact interest & months saved.
- **Smart Diversification Engine**: Adjust your goal (`Conservative`, `Balanced`, `Growth`), tax bracket (`0–30%`), and home loan rate (`% p.a.`) to generate tailored rebalance plans backed by live yields.
- **Maturity Radar & Tax Audit**: Catch matured/expiring FDs, SGBs, and Bonds across 30/60/90 days, track Section 80C / 80CCD(1B) NPS headroom, and harvest annual equity LTCG exemptions.
- **Zero Server Storage**: No sign-up, no accounts, and zero cloud tracking. Import Excel (`.xlsx`), CAMS/KFintech CAS, or CSV and export printable PDF reports or spreadsheets anytime.

### Maker First Comment (Copy & Paste on Launch Day)
Hey Product Hunt! I’m Prateek, maker of **Vestora Wealth**.

Every time an FD matured or surplus cash piled up, I hit the same wall:
- The bank offered a low-yield FD renewal (yielding ~4.5% after 30% tax).
- Govt bond & NPS rules lived in separate portals.
- Fund distributors pitched Regular NFOs with 1.8% commissions while ignoring an active 8.55% home loan.

So we built **Vestora Wealth** — and launched it with an **interactive 2D Street Story Game & short movie** right on the homepage so anyone can experience the problem in 30 seconds before stepping inside the live workspace.

Everything runs **100% on your own device** using live public market feeds (AMFI, Kuvera Direct MF rankings, `npsnav.in`, GoI G-Sec yields, CoinGecko Gold INR, U.S. Treasury Fiscal Data, and ECB FX) — with full Excel/CSV/PDF import and export.
